# Ajora invited-pilot operating brief

**Proposed for Product lead approval · 14 November 2026, Africa/Lagos.** Source inspection used local baseline `ff3d4bfc789c0884b00d3677532502f46926b42c`. Any rehearsal should record its own environment, exact application and schema versions, time, scoped test identities, result, and evidence link. Track ownership and go-live decisions in [pilot readiness](PILOT_READINESS.md) against [section 11 of the ownership plan](IMPLEMENTATION_AND_OWNERSHIP.md#11-release-gates-and-proof-of-completion).

## G01 · One-page scope and limits

| Decision | Proposed pilot scope |
| --- | --- |
| Participants | Existing Nigerian ajo/esusu groups with established rules and members who already know each other. The organiser has completed a previous cycle with the group. The Growth and research lead validates this segment before recruitment. |
| Operator | The operating entity and Product lead are pending assignment. A designated Trust and support lead and backup cover onboarding, disputes and reminders. The Platform operator and backup own availability, data recovery and rollout. Each group designates its existing organiser and a second member to check that organiser’s own contributions. |
| Limits | Start with up to 3 concurrent groups and 3–5 members each, including the organiser. Weekly contributions of ₦1,000–₦5,000 per member per round (100,000–500,000 kobo), maximum pot ₦25,000 per round and 15 participants overall. Prefer one circle per organiser and per collection account. At most one cycle per group until the first evaluation. These are staffed enrolment limits. The Product lead checks each circle at admission against measured capacity and records approval before invitations are sent. |
| Experience | Members pay using their existing agreed Nigerian transfer channel. Organisers compare recorded claims with actual external payments. Only the designated recipient confirms receipt and advances the payout turn. Ajora records the group's assertions and decisions. The bank or payment provider handles transfer errors and reversals. |
| Access and enabled features | Invite-only enrolment with password sign-in and verified contact proposed under I01. Use a separate environment with real-circle creation enabled only after Product approval and tested access controls. Keep simulated receipt tools away from manual circles and label demonstrations. Public analytics, if enabled, uses reviewed synthetic fixtures. No payment execution, lending, guarantees, automatic reminders or live bank verification is offered in this pilot. Enable Google only after I02 acceptance. |
| Support and escalation | Use the approved private support channel published at onboarding and in the app. Proposed coverage is Mon–Sat 09:00–18:00, with scheduled payout windows inside staff coverage, all in Africa/Lagos. Share the channel, hours and backup before members agree. For disputed or stalled payments, follow the case process below and contact the relevant transfer provider through its official route when needed. |
| Pilot end | Review after one complete weekly cycle, or earlier on a stop condition. Any extension or additional circle requires a recorded Product lead decision based on G05, C02 and funded capacity. |

Suggested member wording for approval: “Ajora keeps a shared record for your existing savings group. Use your group’s agreed payment channel. Your organiser reviews each contribution, and the payout recipient confirms receipt. Contact [approved support channel] for a record problem and your payment provider for transfer help.” Check the landing page, sign-in, invitation, agreement, status labels, prepared messages and export together using the release build.

## G02 · Observe organisers before recruiting

Recruit 5–10 organisers across several existing groups for a 30–45 minute observation of a recent, ordinary reconciliation session. Ask them to show their existing workflow in WhatsApp, a notebook or a spreadsheet and narrate decisions in sequence. Use dummy or redacted examples by default. Ask for explicit permission before viewing or retaining identifiable financial material, and record the purpose, allowed use and retention under the approved research policy. Invite willing groups to opt into later contact through a separate private list.

- Time from opening the first source to producing an up-to-date contribution list. Record active minutes, number of claims checked, unresolved references, misattributions, corrections, and reminders/support time separately. Include round size and steps observed so later pilot comparisons have similar workload.
- Ask participants to show how they tell two transfers apart, spot a part payment, handle an overdue member or reversed payment, reconcile their own contribution and tell a recipient what to do. Observe a member identifying the next action in the current tools.
- Use one row per observation with study ID, group-size band, current tools, steps, counts, active minutes, anonymised finding and permission status. Rank problems by frequency and consequence. Record segment selection with supporting observations, baseline medians and ranges, and the number of volunteers who agreed to be contacted. Keep contact details separate from findings.

## G03 · Rulebook for member agreement

The Product lead adopts or revises these rules after G02 and records the effective version. Trust and support checks the rehearsal against the release build. Use one case process for exceptions and preserve dated original assertions and corrections.

| Situation | Proposed allowed action and actor | Minimum evidence and escalation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Late or missed contribution | Remind privately after the due time. Allow completed instalments during a 48-hour grace period. After grace, the organiser opens a case and contacts the member privately. The circle waits for a fully confirmed pot and any enforced dispute hold to clear. | Keep round, outstanding kobo, status and contact attempt time. Trust and support handles unresolved delay and escalates a stalled circle to Product. |
| Due date and an early payout | Treat due dates as schedule reminders. A payout can occur within staffed hours earlier than due if the current round is fully funded, rights and recipient instructions are checked, and no hold applies. | Keep due date, funding/review timestamps, agreement version and payout evidence. Route exceptions to Trust and support. |
| Fees, overpayments and external charges | No pilot penalties. The sending member or organiser covers their own provider's transfer fee so the payee receives the agreed net amount. Confirm only the agreed obligation. Have the parties agree any excess refund outside Ajora through the transfer provider. | Retain the claimed versus due kobo and corrected record, with a private case for excess and provider outcome. Record any refund separately from an in-app contribution. |
| Duplicate or ambiguous reference | The organiser keeps the claim pending until the actual settled credit can be uniquely identified. Reject an incorrect claim with a concise reason. A member resubmits the correct reference or asks support for help if the bank's reference is ambiguous. | Keep both claims, decision and case ID. Use the allocation check below across circles. Escalate ambiguity to Trust and support before payout. |
| Withdrawal or default | An unpaid draft can be replaced via the lifecycle procedure. Keep active membership, order and terms frozen. The organiser opens a support case for a voluntary exit or missed obligation. | Keep request, timing, final agreement and affected turns. Product decides whether and how the group can continue under the approved rulebook. |
| Organiser absence | Members contact Trust and support. The group waits for an authorised organiser to return or for a Product decision using an available, tested transition. | Keep attempts, outstanding receipts and case owner. Escalate to Product. Keep account credentials individual. |
| Organiser's own contribution | The designated second member independently checks the exact debit and credit evidence through the existing trusted process and privately acknowledges the check before the organiser records it. Use an independent supporting review when the organiser has an interest in the claim. | Record checker, time, round and minimal evidence reference in the case or approved reconciliation log. A self-confirmation in Ajora is an organiser assertion. Disagreement goes to Trust and support. |
| Incorrect confirmation or contested contribution | A member flags the claim and reports the case. The organiser uses the audited correction/review controls for the current unpaid round. Resolve the dispute through its supported workflow after checking the record. | Keep original claim, source, disputed/corrected status, reason, acknowledgements and reviewer. Treat prior rounds as historical cases. Follow the dispute workflow below. |
| No payout received, incorrect payout amount, or mistaken payout reference | Recipient reports the problem before accepting receipt. Organiser checks the actual transfer and asks the payment provider to trace it if needed. Do not send again based on an uncertain app response. Recipient acknowledges only after verifying receipt of the full amount. | Keep original payout assertion and a dated linked resolution/correction with the case ID. Escalate to Trust and support and the payment provider if funds are missing. |
| Reversal or mistaken receipt after a turn advanced | Open an incident case and preserve the original round and its acknowledgements. Product, Trust and support and the payment provider agree remediation under the approved rules. | Record relevant original event IDs, correction date, effect on later obligations and acknowledgement. Pause further external activity for the affected circle while an owner investigates. |

## S02–S04 · Member and organiser runbooks

### Payment instructions and receipt allocation

1. Use accepted rules for the payee's agreed display name, channel, contribution amount, schedule, reference convention and review/correction process. Example reference convention is a group short code, round, member alias and instalment number, alongside the payment provider's unique credit identifier when available. The organiser shares complete instructions privately through the already known group channel. Verify that only joined members receive the release's full rules. Keep account numbers, recipient instructions, contact details and credentials out of rules and invitation messages.
2. During onboarding each member verifies the recipient name through the agreed trusted channel and checks it with their banking app. A payout recipient sends their own instructions only to the organiser through that private channel. For a change, the organiser calls or checks in person using an already known contact to confirm the new instructions. In an unpaid draft, use the supported replacement and fresh agreement path. Active-circle collection changes require a supported reacceptance procedure for the changed instruction and prior agreement history. Escalate and suspend external payments while support checks the case.
3. With each claim, review the actual posted credit for the agreed collection account, amount in kobo, provider reference and date. Assign it once to a circle, round, member and instalment. Keep an approved private allocation log keyed by collection-account alias and stable provider credit identifier. If the identifier is unavailable or collision is possible, keep the claim pending for support. If an organiser or account serves multiple circles, search the same log across every circle before approval and serialize allotments through one designated reviewer. Reconcile the log against the app before sending a payout. See [savings API](../api/savings.py).

### Draft replacement and invite abuse

On a leaked code, accidental join or abandoned draft, Trust and support authenticates the organiser and records draft ID, agreed roster and whether any financial activity exists. The organiser deactivates the old invite through the supported release action and verifies the old invite fails both preview and join. After a payment-free draft is made unusable, issue only the replacement or rotated invitation approved for the known roster. Preserve old versions and show fresh acceptance for the valid draft. If the release lacks a tested transition for the case, keep the affected group unenrolled until the Backend lead can verify one. For an active or stalled circle, log the issue and preserve prior agreements, payout order, claims and decisions. Require Product and Trust and support approval before resuming the external workflow. Use authenticated application operations throughout.

### Disputes and holds

A disputed settled credit, incorrect confirmation, duplicate allocation, claimed reversal or unpaid payout requires review before a new external payout. A member raises the flag in the app and opens a private case with the circle ID, round and claim ID. Trust and support owns the case and checks that the app shows the relevant unresolved state. Refresh just before transferring. Check the current round and any hold again before recording an external payout. Verify the release's server guard, permissions, and flag-versus-payout race in staging before promising a hold to members. A report that won the race may already represent an external transfer. In that case the payout remains tied to its original recipient, and support checks the actual transfer before doing anything further. An open claim for a previous round also requires a staffed review before the external workflow resumes.

The organiser reviews a contribution using the published evidence and correction workflow, with a second member on their own claim. The recipient alone acknowledges a payout after checking the actual full receipt. Trust and support shares the finding and the proposer/reviewer identities with affected members as permitted and records acknowledgement. After agreeing the resolution, the original flag reporter closes the payment case in the app with the agreed reason. Support checks the case and app agree before advising that the group can proceed. A missing participant, challenged resolution, incident across circles, or a claim after a turn completed goes to Product and Security and privacy as appropriate. Use the approved bank complaint/trace channel for transfer investigations.

Include Vercel, Render, Turso, Resend and any enabled Google processing. Map who can access names, circle terms, statuses, references, decision reasons and event history. Document data minimisation and retention for each, and include backup restoration and cross-border processing.

Ask counsel for a decision matrix covering the actual manual-tracking activity and claims, entity, complaint/redress process, lawful handling, member notice and access, vendor arrangements, retention and closure, and incident reporting. Ask them to record which obligations apply, what to file or publish, by whom and by when. Confirm applicability of NDPC registration, audit, DPO and impact assessment where relevant. Retain the signed assessment and completion evidence privately. [NDPC official guidance](https://ndpc.gov.ng/).

The Frontend lead publishes approved terms, privacy and support at sign-up and shows any required notices again before agreement acceptance. Add records of policy versions or member acknowledgement where counsel requires them. The Security and privacy lead verifies the release's disclosures and tests permissions on preview, member view and export. Stop the onboarding process for a mismatch in what is published and what the app exposes.

## G05 and C02 · Measure and evaluate the pilot

Declare the cohort at approval and freeze definitions. Use circle and member pseudonyms inside restricted measurement records. Store flags, counts, timestamps and durations. Leave financial references, private identities, account details, exact amounts and reasons in their original authorised records. A restricted manual tally is sufficient if app events are unavailable. Keep report data and contact lists separately.

| Observe | Count or compute |
| --- | --- |
| Setup and invitations | Approved groups with a unique circle ID and creation timestamp. Organiser records invitations sent and eligible intended participants by pseudonym and time. A generated invite link alone is not a send. |
| Join and agreement | Distinct intended members joined divided by intended invites. Count required participants accepted against the exact agreement version used to start. |
| Activation and repeat use | Activated circle completed current-version acceptance and had a first fully confirmed contribution within one week of its first scheduled due date. Count use again in a later due round and count renewed cycles separately. |
| Payout and cycle | Count distinct turns with recipient-confirmed receipt. Report circle completion only once, at final receipt. Record the number of circles far enough along for each measure. |
| Review, overdue and receipt delays | Per instalment, elapsed time from submission to first decision and open age of pending reviews. Per member/round, compute whether full confirmation occurred by the agreed due time and grace end. Per payout, measure reported-to-recipient-confirmed time and age while pending. Report counts, medians and ranges. |
| Quality, effort and cost | Count mismatched app versus externally verified records per claim reviewed, open and resolved cases, corrections, staff active minutes, and organiser active minutes per comparable round versus G02. Group support minutes by case category. Record why people returned, stopped or needed help with an action. |

Proposed decision at first full-cycle review. Expand only after at least one recipient-confirmed payout in each retained circle and at least two circles using Ajora for two rounds, at least 80% of intended members joined and accepted the final agreement, median reconciliation effort fell by at least 20% at comparable volume, and the team handled at least 90% of reviews within the next staffed day. Require no unresolved money/identity discrepancies or critical/high security findings, plus support effort and cost within funded capacity. For a cohort this small, publish counts alongside every percentage.

Stop new enrolment and affected payout operations for lost or duplicated ledger entries, exposed private data, unknown authority to operate, a failed restore within approved limits, or insufficient staffing/funds. Investigate and report to the accountable owner immediately. Revise and rerun within the same limits if a usability, timing or repeat-use target misses without a stop event. The Product lead confirms definitions and thresholds in advance, reviews section 11 evidence before enrolling anyone, and records the expand, revise or stop decision with the actual dated funnel, organiser baseline comparison, discrepancy and support outcomes, cost per active circle and interview themes. Recruit from the approved G02 volunteers only after the gate passes.

## C01, I05 and S06 · Support, privacy requests and records

**Trust and support lead.** Before release, staff an approved support inbox or form and backup, send a synthetic request through it, and publish the reachable address, operating hours and expected response in the app and onboarding. Use a restricted case register with case ID, category, reported time, requester verification status, affected circle/round or account locator, minimum necessary evidence pointer, priority, assignee, next update due, findings, permitted action and result, member acknowledgement and closure date. Use separate restricted storage for sensitive evidence approved by Security and privacy. Limit access to the case owner and authorised reviewers. Ask for a sanitized record identifier before requesting more evidence.

- **Authenticating a request.** Confirm a current session and control of the registered contact or another approved proof under G04. Match the requester to the specific circle before using member-visible diagnostics. For loss of access, follow I01. Keep passwords, sessions, reset/recovery tokens, provider keys and whole bank statements out of the case log.
- **Triage.** Missing money, a wrong recipient, suspected invite or account compromise, disclosure or unavailable ledger during a payout are urgent. Proposed first reply within two staffed hours for urgent requests and by the next staffed day for others. Open an incident when the case affects multiple groups, send the case ID to the reporter, and give a next update time even when resolution depends on an external provider. Escalate a service failure to Platform, unexpected records to Backend, harmful or illegitimate processing to Security and privacy and counsel, and circle disposition or an absent organiser to Product. Transfer traces or reversals stay with the relevant provider and account holder.
- **Savings export.** Authenticate the requester and verify current membership. Guide them through the member-scoped authenticated `GET /savings/circles/{id}/export?format=text` for a readable statement, `?format=json` for machine-readable history, or `?format=csv` for a spreadsheet-safe field list. Deliver any support copy through the approved access-controlled channel. Verify a sample of exact kobo totals and statuses against app history, including an instalment, correction, payout acknowledgement and demo label. Include all required agreement versions and turns. Confirm anonymous and cross-circle access fail and use the provided escaped spreadsheet form. Record who requested it, verified authority, format, circle, delivery time, and expiry/deletion date for any support copy. Cover correction requests with a new dated decision linked to the original.
- **Data access and closure.** Security and privacy reviews each authenticated access, export, correction, deactivation and deletion request against the approved retention schedule. Separate personal account material from records other members are entitled to retain. Define removal or anonymisation of eligible data, permitted holds and backup ageing. For a deactivation, use a tested, authorised action that ends account access through all enabled methods, revokes sessions and outstanding recovery tokens, and preserves authorised shared evidence. Verify the former account cannot sign in, recover or relink, and a different member can still retrieve the agreed ledger. Include reapplying approved post-snapshot restrictions in the restore procedure. Explain the outcome and retained categories through the private channel. Escalate any missing action to Backend and counsel before promising a deadline or offering closure.
- **Rehearsal.** Walk a synthetic disputed receipt and an authenticated deactivation request from intake through resolution. Include the export, cross-circle refusal, escalation, second-person review where required, private delivery, and acknowledgement. Record environment, reviewed version, timestamps and evidence, then remove synthetic case copies under the agreed retention policy.

## N01 · Manual reminder rota

The organiser checks current Ajora state before sending. Proposed schedule in Africa/Lagos: private due reminder at 09:00 the day before due, check for unreviewed instalments at 17:00 on staffed days, and privately remind an outstanding payout recipient the next staffed day. Trust and support follows the agreed backup rota and handles missed reminders. Use the prepared WhatsApp invite/reminder where it fits, check the specific recipient, and have a person send. Keep payout references, bank account details and dispute text out of messages and group-wide summaries. Record circle, round, purpose, recipient alias, sent time and operator only. Cancel an outstanding reminder after the member's status changes. Rehearse all three with the assigned operator and record receipt by approved synthetic participants.

## I01 and I02 · Hosted identity acceptance

**Recovery (I01).** Platform uses an owned, verified Resend sending domain and scoped sending key, with exact HTTPS `AJORA_PUBLIC_WEB_URL`, matching API/frontend commits, and separate staging settings. Use provider DNS instructions for domain verification. Disable tracking that would expose token links through a redirect. Verify inbox delivery with at least two approved addresses outside the Resend owner's mailbox and record provider status, inbox arrival, elapsed time and outcome. Never capture message links or secrets. [Resend domain verification](https://resend.com/docs/dashboard/domains/introduction).

| Window or failure | Expected staging evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Verification, resend, expiry and reuse | Explicit verification updates the same account. Latest link works, superseded/reused links fail, and an expired 24-hour verification link fails. Opening the link alone changes no account state. |
| Reset, reuse and expiry | Registered and unknown addresses see the same acknowledgement. Latest valid link works once and an expired 30-minute reset link fails. Verify a second session and old password fail, fresh sign-in restores the same ID and circle, and the separate reset notification reaches the inbox. |
| Sender or network failure | An invalid scoped staging sender/key cannot break ordinary password sign-in. Verification surfaces provider failure without sensitive content. Reset request acknowledgement is distinct from delivery. Restore settings, request again and verify inbox delivery. Audit controlled logs for redaction. |

Proposal for Product decision: verify the pilot participant's email during guided enrolment under G04, before the organiser issues the live invite. Existing app verification does not by itself gate membership. Publish the support fallback. A member who can still sign in uses the existing session and opens a case if delivery fails. A member without mailbox access contacts staffed support for approved ownership recovery or remediation. Preserve the original ID, verify any alternate identity proof under G04, and keep their circle informed only as permitted. Do not re-create the member or grant access on a name or email claim alone. See [account email](ACCOUNT_EMAIL.md).

**Google (I02).** Default operational recommendation is to withhold Google from the pilot until verified. For that route, leave both `AJORA_GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID` and `NEXT_PUBLIC_GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID` unset in the pilot target and verify neither the UI nor direct challenge offers it. If Product chooses to offer it, configure the exact staging and production JavaScript origins and review Google Cloud audience/branding/test users. Match the API and built frontend web client IDs. Run the actual popup for a new sign-in, password account linking initiated by the authenticated user, supported browser-only profile upgrade, cross-browser return and post-deployment persistence. Verify matching emails require linking while authenticated, wrong-origin/audience and expired or replayed challenges fail, and the original account and circle persist. Capture isolated test evidence without tokens. See [Google setup](GOOGLE_SIGN_IN.md).

### I04 · Proxy and abuse acceptance

The Security and privacy lead and Platform operator should use two controlled staging clients on different public networks and a third synthetic actor on a shared network. In a fresh rate window, exhaust each tested limit with client A, verify client B can still perform a known-good login, reset, registration and legacy guest action, and separately verify email/actor quotas across clients. ## O01, O02 and O07 · Data recovery

### O01 · Backup and restore

The Platform operator and backup person own the schedule, encryption, access and failure alerts under the approved G04 retention policy. Propose 24 hours maximum record loss and same staffed day restoration (8 staffed hours), with encrypted daily exports outside the source account and a separately protected key. Propose 14 daily copies subject to counsel and budget approval. Test the schedule, run a catch-up after failure, check freshness before release, and alert on a missed copy. Use the documented classic libSQL/SQLite backup method for the actual provider configuration. Retain snapshot UTC timestamp, database alias, encrypted object/version ID, checksum, source commit and schema inventory. [Turso export](https://docs.turso.tech/cli/db/export), [Turso point-in-time recovery](https://docs.turso.tech/features/point-in-time-recovery).

1. First seed synthetic staging records through the API, including password accounts, supported account links, member acceptance/history, a payment decision and multiple instalments and a recipient-confirmed payout. Include simulated receipt allocation in test-only demo records if supported in the staging build. Record canonical document hashes and aggregate counts for all tables with credentials and identity state handled privately.
2. Take a consistent snapshot. To rehearse the approved export approach, stop new staging writes at the app boundary and use `turso db export DATABASE_ALIAS --output-file /secure/private/snapshot.db` in an approved encrypted workspace. Record scope and engine/version, then protect the file immediately. Keep account identifiers and data out of console output and shared evidence. Verify checksums and an integrity check on the private SQLite copy.
3. Restore into an isolated database and deploy the same app/schema in an isolated environment, with different secrets, no test mail to actual members, and no production endpoint connected. For classic libSQL verify current compatibility of `turso db create RESTORE_ALIAS --from-file /secure/private/snapshot.db` or use the existing plan's supported point-in-time copy procedure. Start with existing snapshots and no writes from member browsers. [Turso database creation](https://docs.turso.tech/cli/db/create).
4. Compare users, credential continuity, account links, memberships, agreements/acceptance versions, instalments, corrections, allocations, payouts and event history. Log in as each synthetic actor and re-read the record. Assert expected post-snapshot changes are excluded for a chosen point and confirm the operator can reapply approved changes made after that point before restoring access.
5. Record snapshot age at incident simulation, elapsed time from declaration to usable verified service, exceptions and disposition. Preserve the signed drill record, destroy unneeded copies under policy, and repeat after material schema, credential or backup changes. Keep the existing operator backup allocations in scope even when the provider offers point-in-time recovery.

## O03 and O04 · Monitoring, incidents and release

**Readiness and alerts.** Monitor liveness and storage readiness independently. Poll `/api/engine/health` through the deployed Vercel path and `/ready` against the candidate API. Provision a fresh staging database through the ordinary savings setup and a controlled synthetic registration before expecting readiness. The probe reads the existing schema. Add synthetic staged checks for performance and password flow if approved. Capture operation categories, request identifiers, status, latency and error class. Exclude headers, query data and user inputs from logs. Test redaction of emails, cookies, credentials, reset tokens and payment details. Monitor mail provider failures, storage quotas, backup age and cold-start timing separately. Propose warning at 70% of any shared plan quota, escalation and enrolment pause at 85%, urgent alert on three readiness failures within five minutes, or on any missing daily backup. Set actual alert recipients and backup delegates, measure monitoring requests and provider costs, then trigger a storage failure and a backup alert in staging. Verify delivery, acknowledgement time, diagnosis from a request ID and recovery notification.

**Incidents.** Platform records incident ID, detected time, affected scope, severity, confirmed/unknown outcomes, owner, last known good snapshot, next update and recovery. Trust and support communicates the impact without exporting ledger data and reminds members to verify external transfers and refresh ledger state before resubmitting. First isolate the failing release or dependency, pause enrolment and affected workflows, and preserve evidence. Update the published support channel on the assigned cadence.
