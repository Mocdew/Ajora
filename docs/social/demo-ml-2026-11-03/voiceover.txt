Your own transfer should not look like new income. Meet Ajora, a financial insights demo for Nigeria.

Emeka banks across four accounts. A naive total says he earned six point nine nine million naira. Some of that money was already his.

Ajora compares amounts, timestamps, payment references and fuzzy name similarity across banks. These features score candidate debit and credit pairs.

The Hungarian algorithm selects pairs globally. Each transaction can be matched only once. The current demo uses explainable rules for confidence scoring.

High confidence matches apply automatically. Uncertain pairs stay in cashflow until confirmed, and users can inspect or reject any match.

Eight internal transfers are removed. Income becomes five point six million naira. Transfer fees remain in spending, so net cashflow stays the same.
